      What this is, and is not

      This is an information tool. It reports what a versioned, source-cited requirement catalog says may apply to a product, and what evidence is missing. It is not legal advice, not a certification, and not a guarantee of compliance. Every requirement shown links to its primary source so you can check it yourself, and you remain responsible for what you place on the market.

      Where we cannot tell whether a requirement applies, we say so rather than guessing. "Undecidable" never counts as ready. Catalog version __CATALOG_VERSION__. Privacy notice.
