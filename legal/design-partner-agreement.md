## 1. What this is

You get early access to CFM to track your products' EU and UK compliance requirements, store the
supporting documents, and build technical files. In return you tell us what works and what does
not. This agreement covers what we may do with your data and how either of us can stop.

## 2. Cost

Free during the early-access period. You are not committing to buy anything. If we later offer
paid plans, you choose whether to move to one, on terms we will send you before the period ends.

## 3. Your documents and data

1. Our [data processing agreement](https://compliancefilemanager.com/dpa) (the "**DPA**") forms
   part of this agreement. You are the controller of personal data in what you upload; we are
   your processor. If this agreement and the DPA conflict on personal data, the DPA wins.
2. We use your documents and data **only to provide the service to you**. We do not sell them,
   share them, or use them for anyone else.
3. **No training.** Neither we nor any of our providers use your documents to train AI models.
4. We look at your documents or data only to support you when you ask, to investigate a
   problem you reported, or to keep the service secure — and tell you when we have done so.
5. We will not use any of your documents as a test case for improving the service unless you
   give written permission for that specific document. You may withdraw it at any time.
6. Please upload only what the service is for — compliance documents and product details. Do
   not upload special-category data (health, beliefs and the like) or criminal-offence data.

## 4. Feedback and publicity

1. We may use your feedback to improve CFM without owing you anything for it. We will not
   attribute feedback to you publicly without your permission.
2. We will not name you, or show your logo, as a customer without your written permission.

## 5. Early-stage software

1. CFM is **an information tool, not legal advice**. It helps you organise and check your
   compliance evidence. It does not certify products, and we are not your Responsible Person,
   authorised representative, notified body or other economic operator under any product law.
   You remain responsible for your products' compliance and for keeping the records the law
   requires of you (including GPSR's ten-year retention).
2. It is early software. It may change, be unavailable, or contain errors. Keep your own copies
   of your original documents.
3. *Reviewer:* warranty disclaimer wording.

## 6. Confidentiality

Each of us keeps confidential what the other shares that is marked confidential or obviously so
— your documents and business information; our unreleased product plans. This survives the end
of the agreement for [three] years, and for personal data, for as long as we hold it.

## 7. Ending it

1. Either of us may end this agreement at any time by email, with no reason needed.
2. When it ends, we offer you an export, then delete all your data within 30 days and confirm in
   writing when it is done — as set out in DPA §8.
3. Sections 3 (for as long as we hold your data), 4, 5.1 and 6 survive.
